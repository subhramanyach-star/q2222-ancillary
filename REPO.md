This repository is the public ancillary certificate archive cited in the data-availability statement of:

Yutian Wu, Xie Sicheng, and Ravishankar Ramanathan, *Explicit construction of the normal cone at every nonlocal extreme behaviour of Q_2222*.

It contains the code, certificates, and verifiers for the exact polygon certificates, randomness bounds, CHSH variants, and efficiency-interval coverage. Start with `MANIFEST.md` and `VERIFY.md`. This is not the paper source.

The existing `README.md` and `HASHES.md5` are those of the certificate bundle and are left unchanged so the checksums remain valid.
